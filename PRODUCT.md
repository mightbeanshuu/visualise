# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Students preparing for interviews and early-career developers who want visual, project-led explanations of DSA and software engineering topics.

## Product Purpose

Visualise is a learning portal for interactive algorithm visualizers and upcoming engineering tracks. It exists to turn abstract topics into visual flows, code traces, and project-based lessons that learners can revisit quickly.

## Brand Personality

Technical, direct, kinetic.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic course-directory layouts, static text-heavy notes, decorative visuals that do not explain the topic, and marketing pages that hide the actual learning surface behind a hero.

## Design Principles

- Show the system before explaining the syntax.
- Keep each track visually distinct while preserving the dark glass interface.
- Make every diagram earn its space by teaching a flow, dependency, or mental model.
- Teach through projects when a concept needs architecture to make sense.
- Preserve reduced-motion support for animated learning surfaces.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target readable contrast on dark surfaces, keyboard-visible focus states, responsive layouts for mobile learners, and reduced-motion alternatives for canvas and SVG animation.
